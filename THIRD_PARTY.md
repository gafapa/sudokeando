## Nunito Sans

- Copyright 2016 The Nunito Sans Project Authors.
- Project: https://github.com/Fonthausen/NunitoSans
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; full text in [public/fonts/OFL.txt](public/fonts/OFL.txt).
- Distribution source: Google Fonts, Nunito Sans v19, normal style, variable weights 400-800, Latin and Latin Extended WOFF2 subsets.
- Source stylesheet: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Nunito+Sans:wght@400..800&display=swap
- Local files: `src/assets/fonts/nunito-sans-latin.woff2` and `src/assets/fonts/nunito-sans-latin-ext.woff2`.

The font files are distributed with the application and included in the service-worker precache. The browser does not contact the upstream font service at runtime. The font binaries are unmodified.
